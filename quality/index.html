Test equipment in the lab
GSM gaugeWeighs a cut sample and gives its substance in grams per square metre, which is how film weight is checked against the approved trial.
Tensile testing machinePulls a strip of film, or a seal, until it breaks. This is where tensile strength, elongation, seal strength and bond strength are measured.
Hot air ovenHolds laminated samples at temperature so the adhesive is fully cured before the bond is tested.
Pouch vacuum testerHolds a pouch under vacuum under water. Bubbles show where a seal is leaking, so a pack is checked before it is filled rather than after.
Odour test equipmentChecks the film for any odour that could carry into the packed product. This matters on food packs, where the pack must not taint what is inside it.
Checked in production
Film thicknessChecked on every lot against the thickness recorded on your approved trial
Seal strengthChecked on every lot of sealed packs
Bond strengthChecked on every lot of laminated film
