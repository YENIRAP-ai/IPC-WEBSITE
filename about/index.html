About Industrial Packaging Company

Built over time. Focused on what comes next.

Established in 1994, Industrial Packaging Company (IPC) is a flexible packaging manufacturer based in Rai, Sonepat, Haryana, with our office in Delhi.

Over the years, we have built our business around a straightforward approach: understand our customers' requirements, maintain consistent standards, and deliver on our commitments.

Today, we manufacture flexible packaging, multilayer films, laminates, pouches, sacks and other formats.

Our mission

Our mission is to manufacture packaging with consistency, quality and responsibility.

We believe lasting business relationships are built through clear understanding, dependable execution and attention to the details that matter.

Our vision

Our vision is to build IPC as a growing and professionally managed packaging company, while strengthening our capabilities and continuing to develop long-term customer relationships.
