Our product range

From film on the reel to finished pouches and sacks. The thickness and width ranges shown are our normal working ranges; the exact specification for your job is fixed on a trial reel that you approve.

Flexible Packaging

Finished pouches and sacks, converted to your specification.

  • Gauge15–200 µm
  • Width100–1800 mm
  • UsesRice, flour and pulses
02

LDPE Films

Low-density polyethylene film, supplied on the reel.

  • Gauge15–200 µm
  • Width100–1800 mm
  • UsesHeavy-duty sheeting
03

Poly Films

General-purpose polyethylene film, cut and wound to your machine.

  • Gauge15–200 µm
  • Width100–1800 mm
  • UsesPallet and machine covers

Shrink Films

Polyethylene shrink film for bundling and collation.

  • Gauge15–200 µm
  • Width100–1800 mm
  • UsesBottle and can multipacks

Laminated Films

Two and three-ply laminated film for printed packs.

  • Gauge15–200 µm
  • Width100–1800 mm
  • UsesStaple food and grain packs

Custom Packaging Solutions

Packs made to your drawing, or copied from a sample.

  • Gauge15–200 µm
  • Width100–1800 mm
  • UsesReplacing an imported pack locally
