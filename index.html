Quality

Quality and consistency

Before a job goes into production we run a trial and you approve it. That trial becomes the recorded specification, and every lot we make afterwards is checked against it — film thickness, seal strength and bond strength.

The plant is certified to FSSC 22000 for food safety and to ISO 9001, and is registered under the EPR rules for plastic waste.
